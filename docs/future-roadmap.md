- Mollie subscriptions.
- Sponsor portal.
- Digital sponsor inventory.
- Proof-of-play reporting.
- Local/regional ad network.
- Matchday Autopilot.
- Sportlink/Twelve production integrations.

- Native wrappers or certified devices.

- Advanced scheduler/dayparting.

## Do not build before MVP proof

- Canva-like editor.
- Programmatic ads.
- Camera-based audience analytics.
- Multi-zone layout engine.
- Native app store releases.
